Dear Parikshita,

36 YEARS OF BEING WONDERFUL

Thirty-six years of a light so true,
The world grew softer when it met you.
Your kindness enters before you speak
And leaves a warmth in the hearts you seek.

You carry no mask and you keep no score,
You simply give, then give some more.
A gentle soul with a courage rare,
Who makes the heaviest day feel fair.

So here's to your laughter, here's to your grace,
To the quiet strength in your smiling face.
If goodness ever needed a name,
It would answer to yours, and be glad it came.

May this new year bring you all you're due,
Every joy that's been waiting for you.
Best of hearts and best of souls,
You make the world feel whole.

Happy Birthday, Parikshita.
The best girl in the world has her day,
and the world is lucky that you came its way.

With all good wishes
